v6.9.10

Fixed DNS-over-HTTPS, which previously never connected (wrong certificate pins plus a response-read bug) and silently fell back to plaintext — so leak tests showed a plaintext resolver even with DoH enabled. DoH now works and is verified on-device.
Encrypted DNS now fails closed: if it can't reach an encrypted resolver it stops resolving instead of leaking to plaintext.
DNS provider and custom upstream changes now apply immediately while protection is running, no restart needed.
